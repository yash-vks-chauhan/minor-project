===================================

A snapshot of the original project inputs, scripts, manuscripts, and results is under `baseline/`, with a separate SHA-256 manifest. The original datasets are not overwritten.

The official population URL recorded in the report and national-government references is `https://www.mohfw.gov.in/sites/default/files/Population%20Projection%20Report%202011-2036%20-%20upload_compressed_0.pdf`. The mirror is a copy of the ministry-authored document, not a new population estimate by the hosting website. Source PDF page references and table identifiers are included in both extracted CSVs.

The revised complete-case sample contains 22 states. The six excluded states are Arunachal Pradesh, Goa, Manipur, Meghalaya, Nagaland, and Uttar Pradesh, owing to an absent or dashed FY2021-22 e-waste value in the source table. Missing values are not interpreted as zero recycling. `data/sample_coverage.csv` makes every inclusion decision explicit.

Thirteen of the sixteen legacy UPI values differ from their currently published map values. Examples are Andhra Pradesh 83 to 122, Gujarat 107 to 76, Haryana 109 to 136, Kerala 147 to 112, and Tamil Nadu 133 to 107. All revised values are based on observed state-name/value popups, not geographic guesses. The source did not provide a revision history; this audit does not establish why the legacy transcription differs. The newly verified official e-waste table supplies additional states omitted by the legacy spreadsheet. Nine legacy NSDP observations differ from the replacement table's specified vintage. `results/input_changes.csv` records all differences.

There are seven analytical input tables from six source families: TRAI, Dataful/NPCI, MoEFCC/CPCB, NITI Aayog, Ministry of Finance/state statistical directorates, and MoHFW/Registrar General. The paper must not call these seven independently verified government organizations. UPI is explicitly secondary; population and urban share are projections; the datasets have mixed reference years. The monthly national UPI spreadsheet is contextual and is not required for the state analysis.

E-waste tonnes per million people = FY2021-22 collected/processed tonnes divided by projected July 2021 population in millions. This measures activity recorded in the state, not necessarily waste generated by its own residents, household behavior, or a recycling rate. Inter-state material transfers and informal processing are not measured. UPI per-capita estimates exclude unclassified state transactions; that limitation is carried into the revised manuscript.

The min-max index is cohort-relative and only descriptive. Its weights are renormalized legacy non-income weights, documented before rerunning models. Shared NSDP components have been removed. The overall SDG composite still measures broad development and can overlap conceptually with digital development; a robustness check using e-waste alone is therefore required. Prediction uses the unnormalized SDG target and log-transformed e-waste intensity, not the full-sample-normalized index.
